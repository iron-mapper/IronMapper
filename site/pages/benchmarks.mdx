## Where the numbers come from

a ratio slightly below 1 does not mean faster than hand-written code (see the methodology below).

## Reproduce it

One machine, nothing else running, about 2 h 45 min:

```powershell
cd benchmarks
./bench.ps1 full          # menu: ./bench.ps1; Linux/macOS: ./bench.sh
```

The scripts (`scripts/run-all.ps1` and the single steps) are the source of truth; the CLI only drives them.
Details, the smoke check that CI runs and the quick A/B of two versions:
[Run it yourself](../../benchmarks/README.md#run-it-yourself) and [How to run](../../benchmarks/README.md#how-to-run).
